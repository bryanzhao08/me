01 / 05 · RESEARCH
dual-model distillation (dmd)
edge-cloud action classification
arxiv:2410.12165 video-language edge-cloud

authored research on optimizing large video-language models (vlms) for resource-constrained edge hardware. proposed dual-model distillation (dmd), an unsupervised data generation framework to train a lightweight switcher model that dynamically predicts edge model uncertainty and selectively offloads action classification to cloud models — cutting latency and compute while improving accuracy.

model distillation pytorch hybrid edge-cloud action classification zero-shot vlm
read research paper [arxiv] →
02 / 05 · COMPUTER VISION
ai fall detection &
proactive care planning
computer vision vlms & llms real-time

an end-to-end ai-driven surveillance system utilizing vision-language models (vlms) and llms to detect falls and proactively plan senior care. achieved a 92% f1-score by combining real-time video monitoring (video-llava), audio transcription, and segment anything model (sam) for environment 'safe zone' segmentation — drastically reducing false positives without requiring wearable devices.

gpt-4o & video-llava segment anything (sam) raspberry pi edge inference rtsp stream
read research paper [pdf] →
03 / 05 · ROBOTICS
95071X · 2025 worlds robot
tipper latch & mobile goal mechanics
worlds semifinalist onshape cad manufacturing

developed the first robot in the world to introduce the "tipper latch" — hooking onto mobile goals to snap them out of opponents' pneumatic clamps and tight corners. led cad engineering, custom cnc polycarbonate/aluminum parts manufacturing, and autonomous routines. advanced to world championship semifinals (lost in double-dq replay by 2 points).

tipper latch pneumatics parts manufacturing onshape cad auton odometry
worlds recap ↗ season recap ↗ design reveal ↗
04 / 05 · ROBOTICS
95071X · 2024 worlds robot
transmission winch & world record climb
world record skills transmission pto hybrid climb

engineered the only robot in the world featuring a transmission-powered winch that hung to the highest tier (h-tier) alongside a cross-bar c-tier hybrid climb. designed power-takeoff (pto) gearboxes, custom intake geometry, and sub-second elevation triggers. achieved the season world record in driver skills.

pto transmission winch climb fusion 360 c++ / pid driver skills wr
world record skills ↗ worlds teaser ↗
05 / 05 · AUTONOMOUS CONTROL
autonomous aircraft taxiing
in a simulated environment
autonomous control x-plane 12

explored autonomous aircraft taxiing in x-plane by developing a closed-loop perception-estimation-control pipeline. trained a 5-layer fully-connected neural network on a 20,000 image dataset to estimate crosstrack and heading errors from nose-mounted camera feeds, which were fed into a pid controller to continuously maintain collinear alignment with the runway centerline.

x-plane 12 neural network pid control state estimation computer vision
